# Dusk for Mac

DRAFT, not public yet.

Dusk warms your screens after sunset and cools them back at sunrise, on every display, on a schedule you set. It lives in the menu bar.

- Profiles for day, evening, night and sleep, each with its own color temperature and brightness
- Follows sunrise and sunset in your city, with gradual fades
- Different settings for each screen
- Eye breaks, dithering control and other display health tools, all optional
- Pause or wind down to sleep from the menu bar

## Download

Get the latest `Dusk-<version>.dmg` from [Releases](https://github.com/nicholasurban/dusk-releases/releases/latest). Open it and drag Dusk to Applications.

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Dusk is signed and notarized by Apple and updates itself. It asks before installing an update.

## Privacy

Your location, schedules and settings stay on your Mac. Dusk sends optional crash reports with no personal data, and you can turn them off. Details: [privacy](https://nicholasurban.github.io/dusk-releases/#privacy).
